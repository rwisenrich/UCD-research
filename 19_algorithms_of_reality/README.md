# Algorithms of Reality / Universe Engine

This branch is the discovery and theorem-reconciliation layer of UCD. It is distinct from `16_ucdos_native_os/`: UCDOS is the native operating-system/runtime implementation; Algorithms of Reality is the research engine that searches, classifies, tests, repairs, and preserves candidate laws.

Start here:

- [`01_DISCOVERY_LOOP.md`](01_DISCOVERY_LOOP.md) — intake, candidate generation, null tournament, retest, promotion/demotion, preservation.
- [`02_OPERATOR_CARTOGRAPHY.md`](02_OPERATOR_CARTOGRAPHY.md) — multi-representation operator search and branch mapping.
- [`03_LAW_LEDGER_AND_PROVENANCE.md`](03_LAW_LEDGER_AND_PROVENANCE.md) — the shared exact source-law ledger and duplicate/version control.
- [`04_PROMOTION_AND_NO_CHEAT_RULES.md`](04_PROMOTION_AND_NO_CHEAT_RULES.md) — theorem-promotion gates, controls, and failure handling.
- [`05_FORENSIC_RECOVERY.md`](05_FORENSIC_RECOVERY.md) — how the historical 648/1,115-item lineages are reconciled into canonical mathematical objects.

The branch preserves exact theorems, conditional results, countermodels, failed branches, repaired branches, harness-only results, and unresolved candidates as different statuses rather than flattening them into one success/failure label.

The central workflow is

$$
\boxed{
\text{intake}
\to
\text{formalize}
\to
\text{generate}
\to
\text{null/control tournament}
\to
\text{retest}
\to
\text{classify}
\to
\text{preserve}
\to
\text{reuse}
}
$$

with every promoted result tied to provenance and proof/verifier objects.
