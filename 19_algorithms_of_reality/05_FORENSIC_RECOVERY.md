# 05 — Forensic Recovery and Whole-Lineage Reconciliation

The Algorithms-of-Reality branch also carries the project-recovery method used to prevent newer summaries from erasing older mathematics.

## Historical scale

The August 20, 2026 deep forensic pass records a historical Algorithms-of-Reality ledger of 648 deduplicated entries later merged to 1,115 items. The mounted-byte audit recovered 898 unique byte-deduplicated files and 1,494 duplicate/alias paths, with thousands of equation-led results, verification facts, no-go lines, dependency chains, and repair/supersession records.

These counts are inventory signals, not theorem counts.

## Ten recovery lenses

The reconciliation method uses ten orthogonal passes:

1. explicit-name archaeology — theorem/law/lemma/no-go labels;
2. equation-first archaeology — exact identities even when unnamed;
3. verifier-first archaeology — exact ranks, spectra, residual-zero and deterministic checks;
4. negative-result archaeology — obstruction and failed-family mathematics;
5. dependency-chain archaeology — arrows and prerequisite graphs;
6. version stratigraphy — repaired, demoted and superseded branches;
7. independent recurrence — repeated appearance across independent packages;
8. cross-domain motif mapping — recurring structures across branches;
9. proof-object publication slicing — theorem + equations + verifier + controls + provenance;
10. code/manifest archaeology — implemented results hidden in filenames, tests, JSON keys and package structure.

## Reconciliation target

The final ledger is not produced by simply appending every parser hit. It must:

\[
\boxed{
\text{recover}
\to
\text{normalize}
\to
\text{identify mathematical equivalence}
\to
\text{merge true duplicates}
\to
\text{retain aliases/version history}
\to
\text{attach proof/verifier objects}
}
\]

before publication grouping.

## No-loss condition

The active public corpus reaches reconciliation only when:

- orphan public objects = 0;
- unmapped public source files = 0;
- unresolved duplicate identities = 0;
- historical aliases without provenance pointers = 0;
- promoted computational results without proof/code/verifier pointers = 0.

Historical objects may be superseded, but they remain addressable.
