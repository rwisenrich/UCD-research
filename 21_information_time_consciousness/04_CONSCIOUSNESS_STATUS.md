# 04 — Consciousness: Current Status Boundary

The historical UCD corpus contains observer, self-model, UCD-IIT/Phi, antenna, holographic-memory, and biological-registry interpretations. They are preserved as research lineages, not silently promoted into one established theory of subjective experience.

## What is mathematically available

The current public foundation supplies models for:

- finite relational state and history;
- reconstructive memory;
- subsystem integration and correlation;
- observer-state updates;
- internal self-model recurrence;
- causal influence/ablation tests for a self-model;
- molecular and neural experimental interfaces in the MTC program.

These can support operational questions about information integration, memory, recurrent dynamics, causal influence, synchronization, and behavior.

## What is not established

No current theorem shows

$$
\boxed{
\text{a particular UCD scalar or operator}
=
\text{subjective experience}.
}
$$

Likewise, a high integration score, persistent quantum witness, recurrent neural state, or successful self-model ablation test would not by itself prove a unique metaphysical interpretation of consciousness.

## Experimental bridge

The active empirical route is kept in `10_biophysics_neuro/`: molecular candidate -> transduction -> quantum witness -> many-neuron causal network -> matched classical-energy control -> perturbation/ablation.

The decisive form of test is comparative: if a proposed molecular quantum witness is selectively removed while matched classical energy and downstream stimulation are preserved, does a reproducible computational/network capability disappear?

## Publication discipline

Historical consciousness interpretations remain available in provenance/archive. Current papers should distinguish:

$$
\boxed{
\text{information/causal model}
\neq
\text{demonstrated subjective-experience theory}.
}
$$

This boundary preserves the useful mathematical and experimental work without converting an interpretation into a measured result.
