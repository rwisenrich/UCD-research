# 03 — Identity, Memory, and Observer Models

This file separates the mathematical identity/memory architecture from stronger claims about consciousness.

## Carrier versus identity

A physical carrier and the pattern/state identity encoded on that carrier are distinct objects. In UCD notation, a change of carrier does not by itself establish loss or preservation of identity; the relevant question is whether the defining relational information and causal continuity are preserved.

The project therefore tracks:

$$
\boxed{
\text{carrier state}
+
\text{relational address}
+
\text{history/provenance}
}
$$

rather than treating material location alone as the complete identity key.

## Distributed memory reconstruction

The `84 + detail` architecture supplies a finite model of reconstructive memory:

$$
H_{420}\cong H_{84}\otimes M_5.
$$

Coarse-visible state and retained detail jointly determine the reconstructible state. If the detail is erased, direct compression cannot be lossless.

## Active process versus stored record

A stored description of a process is not the same as the process being dynamically instantiated. The project preserves this distinction when discussing observers, software agents, neural models, and biological systems.

A generic observer-state update may be written schematically as

$$
O_{n+1}=\mathcal F(O_n,I_n,M_n),
$$

where `I_n` is new input and `M_n` is retained memory/history. A self-model adds an internal representation `S_n` of the observer's own state and dynamics:

$$
S_{n+1}=\mathcal G(S_n,O_n,I_n,M_n).
$$

The scientifically testable question is whether changing or ablating `S_n` has a measurable causal effect on later behavior, prediction, control, or integration.

## Identity-preservation criterion

For a reversible transformation `U`, exact state recovery satisfies

$$
\boxed{U^\dagger U=I}
$$

on the relevant state space. More general identity claims require a declared equivalence relation specifying which features must be preserved. UCD does not equate mathematical reversibility by itself with biological or personal identity.

## Status

Carrier/identity separation, reconstructive memory, causal self-models, and active-process/stored-record distinctions are retained as mathematical/computational concepts. Stronger claims about personal continuity or subjective experience remain separate hypotheses unless independently operationalized and tested.
